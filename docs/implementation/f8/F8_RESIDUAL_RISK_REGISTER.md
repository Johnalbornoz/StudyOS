| # | Risk | Severity | Where it lives | Mitigation status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Explicitly out of scope for F8 (not risks — deliberate non-goals per the task spec)

- Overall exam readiness score, predicted exam score, passing/admission probability (task §31/§55 — F9's job).
- The Full Mock simulator itself (task §32 — F7's guard remains advisory, F9's job).
- A full student-facing teaching UI/UX redesign (task §33/§55).
- Institution/parent/teacher final dashboards (task §55).
- Production deployment or any Production environment variable change.
