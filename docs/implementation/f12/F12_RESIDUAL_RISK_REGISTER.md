## Explicitly NOT a residual risk (proven, not assumed, in this run)

- Cross-institution isolation: proven with two real, separately-seeded institutions and a real admin of one denied access to the other.
- Institution-role-alone denial: proven with a real membership that exists but is not `APPROVED`/is `REVOKED`.
- Unique-learner vs enrollment-row counting: proven with a real learner enrolled in two classes within one institution.
- Knowledge/Skill/Competency separation: proven with a real population that has Skill evidence and zero Competency evidence, shown as two genuinely different distributions.
- Curriculum coverage FULL/PARTIAL/UNMAPPED separation: proven with a real F6 fixture containing one of each.
- F9-only readiness (never legacy): proven by directly invoking the legacy service to confirm it is live elsewhere, then confirming F12's own module never references it (structural + behavioral proof, not one or the other alone).
- Zero direct academic-truth writes: proven with real before/after row counts across `learning_evidence`/`learner_skill_state`/`readiness_snapshots`, not source-guard alone.
