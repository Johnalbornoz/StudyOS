# Reviewer instructions -- Canary 250 human calibration (form version 2)

You are reviewing 37 mathematics items written by a model. They are development-test content: none of them is
released, none is approved, and your decision is a calibration label, not an approval for use.

## Before you start
- **Do not consult any AI system** (chat assistants, solvers that explain, translation by a model). A calculator or
  a computer algebra system to check your own arithmetic is fine. A review made with AI help is not accepted.
- Work from the packet. Some items are shown WITHOUT any automated result (blind items). Do not look for the
  automated results of any item elsewhere, and do not open the key file: it is not for reviewers.
- Solve each item yourself BEFORE you read its key.

## What to record for each item (ten independent judgements)
- **academic_correctness** -- Is the mathematics of the item right (stem, stimulus, every statement in it)? Solve the item yourself first. Values: CORRECT, INCORRECT, AMBIGUOUS, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **answer_key_correctness** -- Is the key right -- the keyed option, the numeric key, every final answer and every mark point? AMBIGUOUS when more than one answer is defensible. Values: CORRECT, INCORRECT, AMBIGUOUS, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **construct_fit** -- Does answering it require the concept and the evidence actions stated for the item, with nothing given away and nothing else measured instead? Values: FITS, PARTIALLY_FITS, DOES_NOT_FIT, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **assessment_system_fidelity** -- Does it look and behave like an item of the stated assessment system and component (format, tools, language, conventions)? Values: FAITHFUL, PARTIALLY_FAITHFUL, NOT_FAITHFUL, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **paper_level_fidelity** -- Is its demand at the level of the stated paper? NOT_APPLICABLE when the target is not a specific paper (the school profile). Values: AT_LEVEL, BELOW_LEVEL, ABOVE_LEVEL, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **difficulty_achieved** -- Compare the demand you see with the STATED band (FOUNDATIONAL / STANDARD / CHALLENGE). Values: MATCHES_STATED_BAND, EASIER_THAN_STATED_BAND, HARDER_THAN_STATED_BAND, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **originality_material_duplicate** -- Do you recognise it as a published examination question, or as a duplicate of another item of this packet? Values: NO_CONCERN, RESEMBLES_A_PUBLISHED_ITEM, DUPLICATES_ANOTHER_ITEM_OF_THE_PACKET, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- **wording_clarity** -- Is it clear, natural and unambiguous in its language? Values: CLEAR, MINOR_ISSUES, UNCLEAR.
- **final_human_decision** -- APPROVED: usable as it stands. NEEDS_REVISION: sound idea, must be edited before use. REJECTED: must not be used. Values: APPROVED, NEEDS_REVISION, REJECTED.
- **reviewer_notes** -- What you found, in your own words. Required unless every judgement is the first value of its list.

Judge each one on its own. An item can have a correct key and be far below the level of its paper; say both.
For IB and Cambridge items, **paper_level_fidelity** is your judgement as someone who knows that paper: the
packet states no official level, and no profile in this project is the board's standard.

## Also record
reviewer_id, your qualification, the date and time, the minutes you spent on the item, whether you saw it blind,
and `consulted_ai: false`.

## Time
About 5 to 8 minutes for a closed item, 8 to 15 for a constructed one: roughly five to six hours for the packet.
Stop and say so in the notes if an item cannot be judged in that time.

## What happens next
After your review is recorded, some items go to a second reviewer -- another person. You are not told which, and
you are not shown what any automated system decided about a blind item.
